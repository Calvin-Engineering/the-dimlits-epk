About

A heavy, immersive take on indie and alternative rock.

Formed by musicians with deep roots in Portland’s music scene, The Dimlits are a band built on a love for music that is raw, honest, and alive. Between walls of shimmering guitars, hard-hitting riffs, and relentless rhythms are imaginative lyrics exploring the novelty and mundanity of being human. Under a shoegazey atmosphere tempered with the sounds of grunge and indie rock, The Dimlits carve out their own space in alternative music, sure to leave a lasting impression and connect with the listener through their sonic intensity and emotional depth.
